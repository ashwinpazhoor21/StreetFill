# Deliverable 2: 100 app concepts

## How to read this table

- **Cat** is the category number from the brief (1–50).
- **Use** is expected usage frequency. **Diff** is technical difficulty, 1–10. **MVP** is build time for one engineer.
- "Alternatives → differentiation" shows "—" where I found no credible way to differentiate.
- Rating counts are US App Store counts pulled 14–15 Nov 2026. † marks a figure from a secondary source.
- **Evidence** column:
  - **Gap** = researched; a specific gap is backed by primary evidence.
  - **Contested** = researched; several small entrants, none with traction.
  - **Crowded** = researched; strong incumbents or many entrants.
  - **Trap** = researched; looks attractive, evidence says do not build.
  - **Spec** = speculative; not researched in this round.

Of the 100: 9 Gap, 32 Contested, 30 Crowded, 13 Trap, 16 Spec. The crowded ones and traps are included on purpose: they are the ideas a typical generator would hand you, and the research says why they fail.
